# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial safe Analyze, Export, Verify MVP.
- Support Google Takeout `.supplemental-metadata.json` sidecar format.
- Support Takeout numbered sidecar variants (e.g. `DSCF0787.AVI.supplemental-metadata(1).json`).
- Cross-archive pooling for multi-ZIP Takeout inputs.
- HEIC metadata EXIF support with safe fallback.
